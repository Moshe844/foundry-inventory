# Agent plan verification

Verification is on the draft branch using synthetic business records and disposable PostgreSQL databases. The table follows all 214 jobs in the Agent plan v3 PDF. Every listed plan assignment has an independent policy test. The last column records the operational evidence from this change. A row marked **Policy only** still needs a scenario that enters through StockChief and verifies its stored or external result. Other suites may already exercise parts of the job.

The permitted modes are **Alone**, **Trained** (after an owner rule), **Draft** (a person approves), **Suggest** and **Excluded**. Section 11 governs rows 35 and 213 by owner decision. The owner also requires one tap per Starter shipment for row 60. Pro needs the paid EDI add-on for row 185. Custom purchasing requires contract limits.

Run `npm run test:agent-plans` with Node 22, PostgreSQL and Playwright Chromium to repeat the plan and workflow tests.

The taught-rule allowance is shared across an account’s inventories. Two concurrent attempts to reserve Growth’s fiftieth slot for different warehouses produced one new rule and one rejection. An owner could edit the winning rule and free its slot by removing it.

The activity timeline, purchase and stock audit, and StockChief work history were tested against events 10, 95, 460 and 775 days old. Starter showed the first, Growth the first two, and Pro and Custom all four. CSV export requires Pro or Custom. A Custom owner can issue an inventory-scoped `audit:read` key. An inventory-read key was refused, as was the audit key after downgrade. Underlying business records remain available to authorized people for reconciliation.

The same dated customer orders were tested in the agent’s order lookup, sales summary and customer email evidence. Starter used the most recent 12 months, Growth 24 months and Pro and Custom the available history. Importing old-system history into Custom is a separate workflow.

Owners can set an individual job to Watch only or Ask me first for small count postings, automatic reorders, combining or splitting PO drafts, repeat sales, postage, margin prices, automatic transfers and supplier specifications. Handle routine work is available only within the job’s plan limit and existing authority. An owner changing postage to Ask me first stopped a queued purchase before the simulated carrier was called. Staff changes and stale settings were refused. Per-job controls for other jobs still need integration.

The page 11 authority ceiling also applies to approved multi-step missions. A reproduced gap allowed a known-customer order to run on Starter without its own confirmation; the regression now refuses it while retaining a separately approved owner-directed draft. Growth owner-directed orders require a taught customer, product, delivery and total-value scope. An over-limit value, changed pickup location, new customer and downgrade stop automatic creation. Email/PDF ingestion remains the separate job 52 with its stricter Starter exclusion and Growth draft-only mode.

## Release checks

Rolling-release qualification also runs the exact published `937c89ae` mail worker against a newly queued quote follow-up. Before the compatibility fix, that worker sent the message after the quote was declined because it did not understand the new authority. Scoped mail now uses a registered authority-specific, versioned job: the published worker leaves it unclaimed, and the compatible worker rechecks the current quote and holds it. A confirmed send with a lost job acknowledgement is repaired from its actual provider receipt without sending again; unverified delivery cannot repair a dead job. Existing owner-written email keeps its ordinary delivery path. Deploy a compatible worker for the new jobs to complete; queued mail remains pending while only older workers are available. This check uses a simulated mailbox and disposable records.

The local upgrade simulation starts from publishing-branch commit `85dfa16e` and preserves all 246 existing migration checksums and its capacity guard, adding 45 migrations. An already-approved import completes after the upgrade. Under the v3 Growth allowance, four parallel stock lanes verify 50,000 products, 50,003 stock units and $62,505.25 of recorded stock value. The run including the newest page/index changes, background-removal guards and versioned mail jobs took 14 seconds for analysis and approval and 49 seconds for execution and verification. Timings vary between local runs; these figures do not establish a hosted speed guarantee. Existing owner-entered timed samples remain unchanged through the upgrade. Legacy cost document numbers, including lower-case and long custom references, are preserved and the next ordinary cost number continues correctly. Replay adds nothing; product 50,001 is refused. Workbook-wide reorder points, row-per-location reorder points, product cost privacy and serial-versus-parallel catalogue results passed separate regression tests. A separate low-stock regression fix preserves the speed change's direct-product probes while keeping whole-catalogue scopes set-based; both full Inventory and Customer navigation CI gates passed for that correction on PR 121. These are local qualification results, not a deployment claim or a hosted timing guarantee.
