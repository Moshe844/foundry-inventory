# Complete product source reading — duty 136

Owners can upload a complete original PDF, Word file, UTF-8 text, photo or scan in Product source readings, open an existing filed spec/safety source, or ask the native agent to read a saved original attachment without supplying fact JSON. A typed `import.read-product-facts.v1` job reads every original part and saves a complete review artifact. Source facts cover ordinary product identity, materials, dimensions and units, technical ratings, hazards, PPE, first aid, storage, handling, fire/spill, transport/disposal and qualified regulatory/certification statements. No ten-fact or short-description cutoff applies.

Growth approves each finished batch. Pro and Custom can authorize unambiguous fact saving from the exact original without training after Watch and within current global/per-job authority. Owner, plan, disabled/deleting account, current product/variant/unit identity, source hashes, account rule quota, import lane, document/AI allowance, storage and worker lease are checked again during dispatch and before the final transaction. Source bytes, native physical fields and earlier quoted facts bind approval; changed or conflicting source facts need exact owner confirmation. Stock, prices, terms and unit definitions remain unchanged.

Native product cards show the saved values, original units and exact original quotations. Free-form facts and quotes remain owner-only, including after stock-only session role revocation; existing independently typed native shipping fields retain their established access. OCR-only readings, deleted/struck-out text, Word comments and unlinked notes stay uncertain. A quoted certification or safety statement remains source evidence and does not establish legal compliance.

The shared bounded reader preserves all PDF pages, image/text mixtures and Word body, headers, footers, footnotes, endnotes, comments and visual observations. It fails the whole original when a part cannot be safely represented, including a raster that the PDF decoder would otherwise omit. Current resource limits are 20 MB, 100 original PDF pages/source parts, two million readable characters and a three-minute parser deadline. No source subset is silently declared complete. Original files, full page coverage and complete sourced-facts CSV remain downloadable after downgrade.

The current configured `import_mapping` file rate supplies the original reading reservation (100 AI credits for current Agent credit terms), plus one document allowance unless the source already has its receipt. Every internal chunk uses that funded gateway. Complete source artifacts and paid receipts are reused across product facts and optional missing-field enrichment; no flat per-page or per-fact fee is introduced. The complete artifact/native update transaction commits reserved units; a failed whole reading reverses its new reservations while preserving actual provider expenses. Retries and concurrent requests cannot duplicate the complete action or saved fact set.

Work history and savings count only actual saved facts, once per product/source in the savings report. Queued, failed and unapproved Growth readings count zero. Hours stay unknown until an owner records their own manual/review timing.
